Really enjoyed exploring the ==Construct Factory==. Given Mineru’s theme, it felt really fitting that you are tasked with assembling her “body” by collection five pieces in mini puzzle rooms. 

After a really fun boss battle with the ==_Seized Construct_==, I finally was able to retrieve Mineru’s stone. Getting these stones is always accompanied by a cutscene with some of my favorites themes as the soundtrack. The developers made a slightly different variation for each sage, and Mineru was no different.
