Started Hades II a few days ago. As always, Supergiant Games stuns with gorgeous art throughout the game. The changes to the gameplay require a bit of adjustment compared to Hades, but Melanoë plays very well so far.

I like the addition of the Arcana Cards as well as the magic attacks. The story delivery so far is superb.
